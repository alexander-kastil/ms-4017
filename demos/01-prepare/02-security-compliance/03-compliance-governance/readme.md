## Regulatory position

Copilot is covered by the existing Microsoft 365 commercial commitments. There is no separate Copilot compliance program to buy, and Microsoft's position is that Copilot inherits the commitments already made for Microsoft 365.

The certification list a customer will ask for includes GDPR, ISO 27001, HIPAA, ISO/IEC 27018 for cloud privacy, and ISO 42001 for AI management systems. Microsoft has also committed to complying with the EU AI Act. For a first conversation, the [Microsoft 365 Copilot & Copilot Chat Risk Assessment Quickstart](https://servicetrust.microsoft.com/DocumentPage/4fe5df86-848b-4097-b3fa-4625e2b8e8f2) on the Service Trust Portal is the fastest artifact to hand over.

Copyright is handled by the Copilot Copyright Commitment: if a third party sues a commercial customer over output generated by a Microsoft Copilot, Microsoft defends the customer and pays adverse judgments or settlements, provided the customer kept the built-in guardrails and content filters in place. Microsoft claims no ownership of the output, and makes no determination about whether a given output is copyrightable, because similar prompts can produce similar responses for different customers.

## Mapping the compliance team's questions to Purview

Prompts and responses land in the user's mailbox, which is what makes the entire Purview toolchain apply to them without anything Copilot-specific being configured.

| Question from the compliance team | Purview solution | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Who used AI, with what data, and how exposed are we? | Data Security Posture Management for AI | DSPM for AI in the Purview portal |
| Show me every prompt and response as an auditable event | Audit (unified audit log) | Audit solution, and the AI activities tab in activity explorer |
| Preserve and export AI interactions for a legal case | eDiscovery with content search | Query builder: **Add condition** > **Type** > **Contains any of** > *Copilot activity* |
| Keep AI interactions for N years, or delete them after N | Data Lifecycle Management retention policies | Retention policies for Copilot and AI apps |
| Flag inappropriate or regulated content in prompts | Communication compliance | Policy for generative AI interactions |
| Detect prompt injection attempts and risky AI use by insiders | Insider Risk Management | Risky AI usage policy template |
| Stop users pasting secrets into third-party AI sites | Endpoint DLP | Devices onboarded to Purview |
| Prove control coverage against an AI regulation | Compliance Manager | Assessments for AI regulations |

For Teams chats with Copilot, administrators can additionally use the Microsoft Teams Export APIs to reach the stored data.

## Scoping a policy: the three AI categories

Purview groups the AI estate into three categories, and the grouping decides what a policy actually covers.

- **Copilot experiences and agents**: Microsoft 365 Copilot, Microsoft 365 Copilot Cowork, Security Copilot, Copilot in Fabric, Copilot Studio, Microsoft Facilitator, Channel Agent in Teams.
- **Enterprise AI apps**: Microsoft Foundry, Entra-registered AI apps, ChatGPT Enterprise, Anthropic Claude (Enterprise).
- **Other AI apps**: anything detected as generative AI through browser activity in the Defender for Cloud Apps catalog, including consumer ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Microsoft Copilot consumer, and DeepSeek.

Agents inherit the security and compliance capabilities of their parent AI app. That inheritance is the reason a customer who has governed Microsoft 365 Copilot has largely governed the agents built on it, and the reason a customer who has not governed browser-based AI use has a gap that Copilot policies will never close.

## Retention has a precedence rule

Retention policies for Copilot and AI apps behave like every other retention policy. When more than one policy applies to the same location, the principles of retention resolve the conflict, and data is retained for the longest duration of all applied policies and eDiscovery holds.

This matters more than it sounds. A customer who already retains mailboxes for seven years is, from the day Copilot is enabled, retaining seven years of AI interactions whether or not anyone made that decision deliberately.

## Discussion questions

- A regulator asks for every Copilot interaction touching a named project over the last year. Which two Purview solutions do you use, and in what order?
- The customer has strong Microsoft 365 Copilot governance and no policy at all for staff using consumer ChatGPT in the browser. Which of the three categories covers that, and which control closes it?
- A mailbox retention policy predating Copilot is set to seven years. What did the customer just commit to without realizing it?
